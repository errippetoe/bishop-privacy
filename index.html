BISHOP Orthotics — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

BISHOP Orthotics (“the App”) is a 3D scanning tool for capturing limb geometry used in the fabrication of custom orthotics.

What the App collects

Nothing is transmitted to us. The App has no server, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We (the developer) never receive your data.

What stays on your device

  • 3D scans captured with the device’s TrueDepth camera, including surface color images of the scanned limb.
  • Scan labels you enter: a patient first initial, the first three letters of a last name, a date of birth, and the orthotic type. This information is stored only on the device, protected by iOS data encryption, and is never transmitted anywhere by the App.

Camera use

The App uses the front (TrueDepth) camera solely to capture depth and color data during a scan you start. No face recognition or identification is performed, and camera data is never used for any other purpose.

Sharing

Scans leave the device only when you explicitly share them (for example via AirDrop, the Files app, Google Drive, or email). Where shared files go is governed by the service you choose and your organization’s policies, not by the App.

Deletion

Deleting a scan in the App’s library permanently removes it from the device. Deleting the App removes all of its data.

Health information

If you scan patients, the scans and labels may constitute protected health information under the policies that apply to your practice. The App keeps this data on-device and encrypted at rest; safeguarding the device (passcode, device management) and any shared copies is the responsibility of the practice using the App.
